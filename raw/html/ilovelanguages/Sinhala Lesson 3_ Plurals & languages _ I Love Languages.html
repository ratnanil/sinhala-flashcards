Sinhala Lesson 3 (Plural and Languages)

Duration: 30 mins

Course overview ↗Vocabulary ↗Grammar ↗Useful phrases ↗

Compare singular and plural examples. Use the Sinhala examples below to read, listen where recordings are available, and practise recalling a few items without looking. Work at your own pace and return to difficult examples in your next session.

Plural

Knowing how to create the plural from the singular word can double the vocabulary you already know. It can make it easy to have a conversation about different topics by simply knowing some grammatical rules. For example how to change the word "book" to "books" or "woman" to "women".

Below is a list of singular words and their plural equivalent. I included examples about nature as well as about humans. The table contains 3 columns (English, Sinhala, and Audio). Make sure you repeat each word after hearing it by either clicking on the audio button or by reading the pronunciation. That should help with memorization as well as improving your pronunciation.

Now we will try to implement some of the words above into full Sinhala sentences. That way you will be able to see how both forms are used when embedded in a structure.

Singular vs. Plural in a Sentence

English Sinhala Audio

They speak four languagesඔවුන් භාෂා හතරක් කතා කරනවා
ovun bhāṣā hatarak katā karanavā
I visited one countryමම එක රටකට ගියා

She visited three countriesඇය රටවල් තුනකට ගියා
æya raṭaval tunakaṭa giyā
She has one sisterඇයට සහෝදරියක් ඉන්නවා
æyaṭa sahōdariyak innavā
He has two sistersඔහුට සහෝදරියො දෙන්නෙක් ඉන්නවා
ohuṭa sahōdariyo dennek innavā

Daily Conversation in Sinhala

Finally, a list of sentences about languages, nationalities, and countries plus some bonus phrases. For a complete list of commonly used sentences, please visit our Sinhala Phrases page. Enjoy!

English Sinhala Audio
